Boost with a quadratic background and a region#

Two opt-in keys of acquisition.boost, both off by default:

acquisition:
  boost:
    n: 2000
    start_iteration: 15
    density_filter: true
    background: quadratic   # the network becomes a fixed quadratic plus a network
    region: 2.0             # chains and audit see the network only where the boosts' points reach

They answer what the ten-boost study of 5 October ran into (posterior_density_boost.md): after a few boosts a network can rise far above the best true value in places no training point is near, the audit that repairs those places damages the core, and an evidence over a wide box would be at the mercy of both.

What a boost run does with them#

  1. First boost (start_iteration): the network of the ordinary loop supplies the boost points, as without the keys.

  2. First training that holds boost points: the quadratic is fitted to the training set and this one training starts from random weights. The region is built from the boost points. (It is the first training after the boosts’ start. When the boost at the start found no mode and acquired an ordinary batch instead, that training holds no boost point yet: the quadratic is fitted to the training set as it is, and stays.) The audit searches inside the region, the tempered chain runs inside it (its walkers start inside), and the boost’s own chains inherit that. The starting walkers are drawn uniformly in the region and kept where they are in the box; along a parameter whose posterior fills its range or lies against a bound, the region reaches beyond the box. When 200 draws have not found the walkers (0.46% of the draws inside at network 18 of an axion run, whose farthest boost row had gone from 9.7 to 18.2 sigma), the draw closes in on the region’s centre.

  3. Every later boost: warm start as usual, with the quadratic unchanged; the region is rebuilt from all boost points so far.

What was found, on the two analytic targets#

All numbers are errors of the network’s log-likelihood on exact posterior draws (24 000 at T = 1), on the 58-parameter Gaussian and the two-mode analytic twin of SINU (16 parameters).

The far false maxima belong to the long chain of warm starts, not to the boosts. Networks trained once from random weights on the same data have none, in two whole-box searches each (plain, both targets). Carried through four further boosts with the loop’s own warm start and schedule and no audit at all, they still have none (30 searches). The chain of 16 warm starts had 5 to 11 per search on the Gaussian.

The quadratic background#

model/network.py: QuadraticBackground, fit_background, build_model(..., background=).

The model is q + network, q(θ) = top − ½ (θ − m)ᵀ P (θ − m). m and the covariance C are those of the training rows within T·ndim log-units of the best (T the acquisition temperature), P = κ C⁻¹ with κ the one number that best matches those rows’ depths, top the best value. Deliberately crude, and never refitted: it is stored in the model file as non-trainable weights, and a later training takes it from the previous model. The network’s targets are the log-likelihood minus q; a warm start keeps the function exactly as before.

A least-squares fit (background_fit: least_squares, opt-in, 20 November 2026)#

The crude quadratic is not the best quadratic, even for an exactly Gaussian likelihood: one covariance read off the tempered rows and one scale leave the network a quadratic correction with up to d(d + 3)/2 coefficients. On the 58-parameter Gaussian that correction was 1.63 rms at the posterior, and the networks’ error there was 4 to 6% of it. background_fit: least_squares (fit_background_least_squares) fits the quadratic by least squares instead, with the injection’s fit (dataset/peak_samples.py, _fit_quadratic) to the rows within background_window · T · ndim / 2 log-units of the best, never fewer than three per coefficient: the injection’s rule, with its window of 16 by default. Curvatures that are not negative are flipped and the others kept as fitted. The injection’s floor at 1e-3 of the largest curvature is not applied to them: in standardised coordinates of a correlated likelihood it raises true curvatures, and it left the exact fit of the 58-parameter Gaussian 529 rms off at the posterior. A fit whose stationary point lies beyond the farthest fitted row is refused, and that training has no background, as when the crude fit fails. Like the crude one it is fitted once and never refitted.

The region#

sampling/region.py. Built from the boost rows alone: the T = 1 posterior points whose true values are known and lie within T·ndim/2 of the best (RUN/boost/boost_it_N.csv). One Gaussian per mode, in box units; ρ(θ) is the smallest, over the modes, of the Mahalanobis distance to the mode’s Gaussian divided by the largest such distance among that mode’s own rows; inside means ρ ≤ region. Saved as RUN/boost/region_it_N.npz.

Cloud matching uses verified rows, not mode numbers (which are weight ranks). Each older cloud joins the current group with the largest fraction of rows covered by either cloud’s ellipsoid, provided the two are one mode: the mean of the one with fewer rows must lie within the reach of the other, in that other’s shape. Otherwise it stays as it is, as does a mode that is absent from a boost. An older cloud is assigned once; several old clouds may join one current group when a later boost finds them as one. Distinct groups of the current boost never share historical rows. This geometric association is not a test of physical mode connectivity. (Any overlap at all is not enough: one stray row merged a mode’s 3 or 4 rows into the other mode’s 10000 in 2 of about 620 trials on the twin’s boost files, and that cloud’s reach went from 10.6 to about 29.)

A sparse cloud takes the shape of a well-sampled one. With at most twice the dimension in distinct verified points a cloud has no covariance to speak of. It is given the shape of the well-sampled cloud with the most rows, its own centre, and it reaches as far as its own rows do, never farther than the cloud whose shape it has. Without a well-sampled cloud the shape is the scatter of all the clouds about their own means once that is well sampled; until then, and for a single verified point, the cloud is left out of the region, counted in the log line and in the saved file (left_out), and the run goes on. For deciding whether two sets of rows are one mode a sparse set is taken to be as wide as the cloud whose shape it borrows: its own few rows say where it is, not how far it goes.

Why not a shrunk covariance: shrinkage pulls towards a sphere in box units, and a real cloud is hundreds of times narrower in some directions than in others (principal widths 0.00025 to 0.15 of the box on the 58-parameter target, 0.0017 to 0.2 on the twin).

Modes of one posterior differ in shape, so the borrowed one is an approximation: on the twin a tenth to a quarter of such a region lies outside the region the mode’s own 10 000 rows give. It holds until the mode has its own rows, which is two boosts for a mode that gets its share of the points. A single distinct point must not raise an error, which would come from the saved boost files and so at every restart: it is left out and counted, as above. Well-sampled clouds, and so every single-mode run with its hundreds or thousands of rows per boost, keep their own covariance, and saved region files are not rewritten.

Who sees it, from the second boost on:

  • the tempered chain and through it the boost’s own chains: the surrogate is the network inside and, outside, a value that does not look at the network at all (SurrogateLikelihood(model, metadata, region=));

  • the audit: its searches are projected onto the region (find_maxima(..., project=)), so only maxima a T = 1 consumer could meet are evaluated and trained on;

  • client.py evidence RUN, by default (--region none for the bare network; evidence.md);

  • client.py profiles, by default (the profile setting region, create_profiles.md): the searches for a profile’s optima are given the network inside the region and the wall outside, as a Keras model at double precision (model.network.restrict_model). The training domain and the quantile reach (17 October 2026, opt-in)#

Measured on five runs: it is what the axion run needed, and it costs accuracy on near-Gaussian likelihoods (boost.md, two recommended settings).

The axion monodromy run (24 parameters, Planck TT at 15 sigma) has structure of a few log-units in three of its parameters, the oscillation’s amplitude, frequency and phase, and its networks did not learn it. Two things in the run’s control branch (chain search on, the region at edge 2) pointed at the training set’s dynamic range and at the region’s reach:

  • The far points set the network’s output scale. The network’s output is standardised by the spread of its targets (the truth minus the quadratic): 52 280 log-units over the whole training set of network 17, 22 without the 11% of its points more than 1000 below the best (they reach -2.4 million). msre gives those points almost no weight (its effective number of points was 40 100 with them and without), but an optimiser’s step is fixed in the weights, so in units of the scale: the oscillations were two millionths of it. Those points also sit up to 420 000 above the quadratic, which falls faster than the truth there: the rising correction of “The quadratic background” above, in the directions the region’s wall then stops.

  • The false maxima sat on the region’s wall, and one far point moved the wall. From network 15 on, the 366 false maxima the audits evaluated lay at 2.00 times the reach, the posterior’s own points within 0.8 to 1.0 of it. At network 18 one far boost point took the farthest from 9.7 to 18.2 sigma; the region doubled and took in a false peak (16% of that network’s posterior, about 780 above the best). The 99.9% point of the same distances was 7.3, 7.5 and 7.6 at networks 12, 17 and 18.

train_domain: E trains every boost network (from the first training after the boosts’ start) only on the points inside an ellipsoid: the mean and covariance of the points within T * ndim of the best, the ones the quadratic background is fitted to, reaching E times as far as the farthest of them (training/domain.py). It is built once, at that first training, from the training set there, saved as boost/train_domain_it_N.npz, committed with that training and read by every later one, so that it does not grow with the boosts as the farthest-point reach does. The background, the network’s input and output scaling, the loss’s normalisation, the validation split and the audit’s fine-tunes all see the cut set. The points outside stay in the training set’s files and in its other uses (the acquisition’s density, the derived emulator). In the control branch the domain built at network 9 held, at edge 1, 84% of network 17’s training set with log L from -697 to -408, and the region at edge 1.5 lay wholly inside it at every network (at edge 2, 8.4% of network 17’s region did not).

region_quantile: q makes each cloud’s reach the q quantile of its points’ distances from its centre; which points are one mode is still decided by the farthest point. A sparse cloud’s own reach, capped at its donor’s, is the same quantile.

Both need the region, which keeps every chain, search, evidence and profile inside it: a network trained on part of the box has nothing to hold it down elsewhere. The loop prints, at every boost, an estimate of the uniform-volume share of the box-clipped region (to its edge) outside the training domain. It uses 4000 proposals from the unit box or the inflated ellipsoids, choosing the proposal with the smaller total volume. Ellipsoids are selected proportionally to their volumes; draws outside the box are discarded and a point inside m ellipsoids has weight 1/m. Thus overlaps contribute once to the union volume. The estimate is sum(weight * outside) / sum(weight), not a posterior mass fraction. Unlike Region.start, this diagnostic never moves draws towards ellipsoid centres. The log reports the volume-weight Kish ESS; with no hits coverage is unknown, and below 200 effective samples it is marked poorly resolved rather than assessed against the 1% warning threshold. Otherwise an estimated share above 1% produces a warning. This Monte Carlo estimate cannot prove containment, even when it returns zero. The coverage fractions quoted above for the control branch were measured with equal-cell initialisation draws, which are not uniform in volume, and should not be interpreted as uniform-volume measurements. A branch of a run at its boosts’ start leaves the domain behind and builds its own (the file is named after the network whose training built it).

Without the keys a region reaches to its farthest point: 218 regions of 41 runs’ boost files, built with and without the code for these keys, are identical. tests/test_train_domain.py covers the domain and the reach; tests/mpi_train_domain_smoke.py runs a branch with both keys through the launcher (hpc/launch.py) on two ranks, restarted once. What they do for the networks is in boost.md.

What is not established#

  • The two ten-boost runs below are single trajectories on analytic likelihoods. They show that the loop with both keys runs ten boosts without a false maximum and reaches its accuracy in one or two; they do not establish how many boosts a problem needs.

  • The cold start and the quadratic were introduced together in those runs; what each contributes was measured on single networks only (the tables above).

  • No false maximum appeared in those runs at all, so they did not need the region. That it does its work when one is there rests on the separately trained twin network above.

  • The evidence with the region is validated on the twin only.

  • Profiles in the region are validated on one real 49-parameter run, with the edge at 1, and on the twin with the edge at 2. The run’s own edge of 2 has not been used for profiles on a real likelihood.

  • A search cannot find a narrow false maximum inside the region; there the boost points, which are posterior samples with true values, are the check.

  • The region holds the chains to the modes the first boost found: a boost no longer discovers a mode.

  • On the twin the lowest far maxima (10 to 20 above the best) had come to 2.3 times the reach after three boosts. One that enters the region is the audit’s to find; its repair still fine-tunes at 1e-5, and a cool-down after a repair is not implemented.

  • A warm-started boost at the loop’s rate scatters from one training to the next (0.25 to 0.38 on the Gaussian with the background); restarting at 1e-6 is steady and gains little.

The gain comes at once. After the first boost the centred error is 0.31 on the Gaussian and 0.22 on the twin, against 1.02 and 0.73 at the same round without the keys; the twin is then where the 5 October run arrived after seven boosts. From the second boost on neither run improves by more than its own scatter: 0.21 to 0.31 on the Gaussian with no trend, 0.16 to 0.18 on the twin with a slow gain (0.166 after two boosts, 0.157 after ten). The T = 1 rms follows the centred sd except where a training leaves a constant offset (0.77 at the Gaussian’s third round is an offset of −0.70).

The credible metric has a noise floor there. Scoring the two halves of one chain separately gives, after ten boosts, 0.055 and 0.055 for the Gaussian’s worst 68% metric and 0.032 and 0.037 for the twin’s; at the twin’s fifth round the worst 95% metric is 0.069 in one half and 0.009 in the other, so the 0.070 of that round is chain noise. Below about 0.03 to 0.05 the worst credible metric does not tell two networks apart. The error on exact posterior draws has no such floor and is the better measure of the core.

Audit and region. All 20 audits were clear without a true evaluation; the 5 October runs spent 50 (Gaussian, five boosts, the fifth not clear) and 18 (twin) at the boost stage. A search of the whole box, without the region, finds no maximum above +10 in the Gaussian’s networks 20 and 25 or in the twin’s network 25. No boost point was left out as unverified. The Gaussian’s region is steady (reach 10.3 to 10.6 sigma). The twin’s grows, from 6.9 and 6.2 sigma for its two modes to 8.3 and 10.3 at the second boost and 10.6 and 11.6 by the tenth: a few points of the new, better network lie at up to 1.66 times the first boost’s reach with true depths of only 9 to 16, real posterior the first boost had not reached. The reach is set by the single farthest point; a high quantile (region_quantile) would be the steadier definition.
