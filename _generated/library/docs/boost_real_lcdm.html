The loop with boosts on a real likelihood: ΛCDM, Planck + DESI#

The first runs of the whole pipeline on a real likelihood: ten iterations of the ordinary loop (tempered batches, the optimum audit, injected points), then five boosts with the density filter, the quadratic background and the region (boost.md, posterior_density_boost.md, boost_background_region.md). Two runs on 7 October 2026, each on one 48-core cluster node, identical apart from the injection’s window (injection_window.md).

What they show:

  • Both final networks reproduce the true likelihood to 0.06 log-units rms at 500 independent samples of their own posterior (largest error 0.2). Reweighting those samples by the true likelihood leaves 498 effective points of 500 and moves no mean by more than 0.01 σ and no width by more than 2%.

  • The two runs give the same posterior: no mean differs by more than 0.013 σ and no width by more than 0.8%, below the noise of one chain.

  • The ordinary loop alone had not got there. After ten tempered iterations and 23 400 points the error at posterior samples was 1.0 and 0.7 log-units, and the foreground nuisance posteriors were 14 to 32% too narrow. The loop’s drift metric had stopped falling: between 0.1 and 0.8 from network 5 on, and 0.02 after the boosts.

  • One boost did most of it. After the first boost the error at posterior points was 0.25 and 0.14, after the second 0.13 and 0.12.

  • No false maximum from network 4 (3) on: every later audit, the boosts’ included, was clear without a true call.

  • The restricted injection made no difference to the final network. Before the boosts that run was the better of the two; one seed each.

  • A run took 5.0 hours: 33 100 evaluations in batches (0.9 h on 48 cores) and 303 or 214 audit calls.

The runs#

likelihood

Planck 2018 low-ℓ TT and EE, NPIPE CamSpec TTTEEE, DESI DR2 BAO (all tracers), through Cobaya 3.6.2

theory

CLASS++ at commit 7cc41a51, massless neutrinos (N_eff = 3.045)

parameters

15: ω_b, ω_cdm, H0, ln(10¹⁰A_s), n_s, τ and nine CamSpec nuisances

training box

hpc/runs/lcdm_planck_desi/domain_bounds.json, applied by the launcher

design

2000 Latin-hypercube points + 100 injected

networks 0 to 10

per iteration 2000 points at T = 7 + 100 injected; the audit at its defaults

networks 11 to 15

five boosts of 2000 posterior points: density_filter: true, background: quadratic, region: 2.0; modes by the fixed barrier

network, training

5 × 512, Alsing; msre with the ESS floor, plateau learning rate, warm starts

sampling

AIES, 256 walkers, 100 000 steps

seed

42 in both, so the 2000 design points are the same

The two runs: every point (the injection’s quadratic fitted to every training point, which is no longer a setting) and window 16 (only the points near the best, as CLiENT does now). Both use hpc/runs/lcdm_planck_desi/cobaya.yaml; the settings of the second are hpc/runs/lcdm_planck_desi/client.yaml.

Five of the 2000 design points had no finite likelihood; no later point failed.

How the accuracy was measured#

  • Held-out errors. Network N chose the points of iteration N’s acquisition and had not seen their true values. Network N at those points against the truth is a test where its own tempered posterior is (networks 0 to 9) or where its posterior is (the boost points of networks 10 to 14, which are selected by the density filter and so are not plain posterior draws).

  • The true check. 500 samples drawn at random from a T = 1 chain of a network, with the true likelihood at each: network 10, the last one trained on tempered points only, and network 15, the last. With D = truth minus network: its rms, the effective number of points (Σw)² / Σw² for w = exp(D), and every parameter’s mean and width with the weights w against the network’s own.

  • Posteriors. Every network sampled at T = 1 and compared with the last network of its run, and the two last networks with each other. Agreement between networks is convergence, not accuracy.

Errors are in log-likelihood units throughout. Pairs of numbers are every point / window 16.

The CM68 in brackets is the chain noise of network 15 (two halves of its own chain). The log evidence is the network’s own, by bridge sampling inside the region, and is each boost’s by-product, so network 15 has none.

  • The posterior is within chain noise of the final one from network 12 (every point) and network 11 (window 16): two boosts and one. The error at posterior points keeps falling after that, from 0.13 to 0.06 and from 0.12 to 0.06.

  • The first run’s network 11 still had ln A_s and τ 10 and 11% too wide; its network 12 has them within 3%.

  • The log evidence moved by 1.03 with the first boost of the first run, which is the bias removed (network 10 was 0.82 low at posterior points), and by less than 0.1 afterwards.

  • Every audit of networks 10 to 15 was clear without a true call; the highest maximum the searches found was 0.6 to 1.3 above the best true value (in the window run 4.9 at network 10, before its first boost).

  • Each boost found one mode. All 2000 candidates passed the density filter every time.

  • From the second boost on, 8 to 23 of the 512 walkers of the mode search ended in smaller groups and were left out (the fixed barrier of 4 log-units at 15 parameters).

  • The region held 2000, 4000, … verified boost points, the farthest at 8.1 to 9.5 σ of the cloud, the edge at twice that.

The two injections#

With every point fitted the injected points lay 4 300 to 9 400 log-units below the peak through all eleven injections; with the window they were at the tempered batch’s own depth from the third on (injection_window.md).

At the end there is no difference: both final networks are 0.06 from the truth and the two posteriors are the same. Before the boosts the run with the window was the better one (network 10 at posterior samples 0.69 against 1.03, 220 effective points against 79; network 9 in the band 15 to 52 below the best 2.9 against 3.4), and it stayed about one boost ahead (0.14 against 0.25 after one, 0.06 against 0.10 after three). With one seed each that cannot be told from the scatter between two runs. What is certain is the cost: the first run spent 1100 evaluations where they taught the network nothing about the posterior.

A tempered iteration took 12 to 26 minutes. An iteration that trains on boost points and acquires the next boost took 21 to 22, and 27 with the cold start. The likelihood is under a fifth of the wall time: training and the chains run on one rank while the other 47 wait.

What is not established#

  • One likelihood, 15 parameters, one mode, a posterior close to Gaussian in its cosmological parameters. Two runs, one seed each, which differ in the injection: the scatter between two runs of the same configuration was not measured.

  • The tails of the final network. The true check tests the bulk (median 6.6 below the best). Between 15 and 105 below, the last measurement is network 9’s (2.9 to 7.1); no network after the first boost was tested there.

  • How many boosts are needed, and when to start. Here one or two gave the posterior and five gave 0.06. The loop’s errors had stopped falling fast by network 4 or 5; starting the boosts there was not tried.

  • Which ingredient did it. The cold start, the quadratic background, the region and the density filter were all on. No false maximum appeared after network 3, so the region was not needed against one.

  • The walkers left out of the mode search from the second boost on (2 to 4%) were not looked into. acquisition.boost.mixing (boost_modes_mixing.md), which would join such groups when the chain mixes between them, was off.

  • The evidence. −5540.13 in both runs is the networks’ own, inside the region; it was not compared with a nested sampler.

  • Wall times are from one cluster node and say nothing about another machine.

Files#

hpc/runs/lcdm_planck_desi/ holds cobaya.yaml and domain_bounds.json, the likelihood and the training box of both runs, and the client.yaml of the window-16 run. The launcher that applies the box is hpc/launch.py.
