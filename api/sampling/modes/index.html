sampling.modes#

The modes of a posterior that a T = 1 chain cannot travel between, and T = 1 samples and the evidence of each. For a surrogate network: no true calls.

A tempered chain (the loop’s acquisition chain) visits every mode; its split between them is not a good estimate of their weights at T = 1, and a T = 1 chain stays where it starts. So:

  1. an ensemble started on the tempered chain is cooled to T = 1, and every walker then climbs to its local maximum (projected gradient ascent, as the audit’s search);

  2. two maxima are in the same mode when the straight path between them never dips more than barrier log-units below the lower of the two; the connected groups are the modes, and a walker belongs to the mode of its maximum (groups holding less than min_share of the walkers are dropped and reported). The maxima, not the walkers, are compared: a walker of a T = 1 chain sits some ndim/2 log-units under its peak, which hides a barrier of that size, and one stranded on the barrier would join the two sides;

  3. each mode gets its own T = 1 chain, started on its walkers and kept in its region (the region of a mode: where its Gaussian, fitted to its T = 1 walkers, is the nearest);

  4. the evidence of each mode, the integral of exp(log-likelihood) over its region, by bridge sampling between the mode’s chain and a Gaussian mixture fitted to the walkers of one half of it, bridged with the other half. The weights of the modes are the ratios of the evidences.

With one mode, steps 1 and 3 are a T = 1 chain and step 4 its evidence.

Step 2 by measured mixing (mixing, opt-in). A fixed barrier stands in for “a T = 1 chain cannot travel between them”, and how high a barrier a chain crosses depends on the dimension: measured with this module’s own chain on two equal Gaussians, 512 walkers and 10 000 steps still mix across 6 log-units with 16 parameters and no longer across 3 to 4 with 58. With mixing set, the maxima are first grouped with a low candidate_barrier (dips under it are the network’s own wiggles) into candidate basins, a free T = 1 chain of the same length as the modes’ chains is run over all of them, and two basins are one mode when that chain forgets which of the two it started in: with a = P(in A at t + lag | in A at t) and b = P(in A at t + lag | in B at t), both among the walkers found in A or B at t + lag, the memory a - b is 1 for basins that never exchange a walker and 0 for basins that exchange them freely. It is measured at half the chain’s length and squared (what is left after the whole chain); basins whose memory is at most mixing are merged. The chain, the walkers and the number of steps are the ones the modes will be sampled with, so the answer is about them. Where a walker is, is decided by letting it climb: it is in the basin of the maximum it reaches.

exception sampling.modes.NoModeFound#

No group of cooled walkers reaches min_share: the network has no mode to draw posterior samples from. Seen on a network far from converged, whose surface is so rough that nearly every walker climbs to a maximum of its own.

sampling.modes.find_basins(values, points, barrier, interior=8)#

The candidate basins of the mixing test: points (local maxima) grouped as in find_modes with a low barrier, but nothing is dropped. A stalled climb goes to the basin of the highest point it is joined to (it lies on that hill; left out of the grouping, it cannot join two hills). Returns (labels 0, 1, … by size, number of basins, dips): dips[a, b] is the smallest dip found on a straight path between a maximum of a and one of b.

sampling.modes.mixing_memory(labels, lag, basins=None)#

How much a chain remembers, lag stored steps later, which of two basins it was in.

labels: (stored steps, walkers) basin of every walker at every stored step (-1: none). Returns (memory, counts): counts[a, b] is the number of (step, walker) pairs found in a and, lag steps later, in b; memory[a, b] = counts[a, a] / (counts[a, a] + counts[a, b]) - counts[b, a] / (counts[b, a] + counts[b, b]), 1 when no walker changes between a and b, 0 when where a walker is does not depend on where it was (nan when one of the two was never occupied).

sampling.modes.assign_basins(predict, values, points, peaks, basin, lower, upper, rng, representatives=48, interior=8, chunk=1024)#

The basin each of points lies in: every point climbs to its maximum, and takes the basin (basin, >= 0, of the known maxima peaks) to one of whose maxima the straight path dips least. Up to representatives maxima stand for a basin, the highest among them, so that a long ridge is not judged from its summit alone. Returns (labels, dips): the dip to the chosen basin, large when the point is on a hill none of the known maxima is on.

class sampling.modes.Regions(clouds, lower, upper, groups=None)#

The box divided among the modes: a point belongs to the mode whose Gaussian (mean and covariance of its T = 1 walkers, in box units) is nearest in Mahalanobis distance plus log-determinant. Between well separated modes the boundary runs through the barrier, where there is no posterior to speak of, so its exact course does not matter.

sampling.modes.bridge_log_evidence(x, f, values, inside, lower, upper, components, rng, n=20000, widen=1.3, repeats=3, walkers=None)#

log of the integral of exp(values) over the region inside, from samples x of exp(values) there (f = values(x)): bridge sampling (Meng and Wong) against a Gaussian mixture fitted to one half of the samples, widened, and cut to the region, with n samples of the other half. Returns the mean over repeats halvings, their spread, and the efficiency of the mixture as an importance proposal (1: it is the posterior; near 0: do not trust the number).

The mixture must not have seen the samples it is bridged with: a mixture fitted to points lies higher at them than at other draws of the posterior, and the estimate comes out low. For x from an ensemble chain, pass walkers, its number of walkers, with the rows as chain().reshape(-1, ndim) gives them (stored step, then walker): the mixture is fitted to the walkers of one random half and bridged with the others, a new halving at every repeat (one walker: the first and the second half of its steps). Random rows, the halves until 21 November 2026, share their points where the autocorrelation time spans many stored rows: on two 44-parameter Euclid networks (512 walkers, 10 000 steps, an autocorrelation time of 675 steps) they gave ln Z 0.27 and 0.26 below halves by walker, halves by time and importance sampling (docs/boost.md, “Weights”). Without walkers the rows are taken to be independent draws and halved at random, as before.

sampling.modes.mode_log_evidence(k, *arguments, **options)#

sampling.modes.posterior_modes(predict, lower, upper, tempered, temperature, *, barrier=4.0, min_share=0.02, walkers=512, cool_steps=2000, steps=10000, components=6, seed=None, log=print, mixing=None, candidate_barrier=1.0, mode_depth=None)#

The modes of exp(predict) inside the box, with T = 1 samples and the evidence of each.

predict: TensorFlow function (n, ndim) -> (n,). tempered: positions from a chain at temperature (at least walkers of them are used). Returns (modes, info): modes is a list, heaviest first, of dicts with x (samples, float32), f (predict at them), log_evidence, log_evidence_spread, efficiency, weight, walkers; info has the temperatures, the number of walkers dropped and the shares of dropped groups.

mixing: None (the default) groups the maxima with the fixed barrier. A number in (0, 1) decides instead by a free T = 1 chain between candidate basins (candidate_barrier): two basins are one mode when the memory left after steps steps is at most mixing; info then has the test under “mixing”.

mode_depth: None (the default) keeps every mode. A number of log-units: a mode whose highest maximum lies more than that below the highest mode’s is left out before its chain is run, and counted in info[“deep_modes”] (acquisition.boost.mode_depth). In the axion run the first boost of a branch, searched over the whole box, found a “mode” 1030 below the posterior’s, in a corner where the likelihood refuses every point, and spent half the boost there. “auto”: temperature * ndim / 2, the depth of the tempered chain’s own reach (84 for the axion run’s 24 parameters at T = 7).
