sampling.region#

acquisition.boost.region: the part of the box that the posterior points of the boosts reach.

A network is only as good as its training points allow, and far from them it can rise above the best true value (training/audit.py). Whatever uses the network at T = 1 (the chains of the next boost, a nested sampler, a profile) has no business there: a region that no posterior chain touches carries no posterior weight. The region makes that a property of the surrogate instead of something every consumer has to hope for.

It is built from the boost rows alone, the T = 1 posterior points whose true values are known (RUN/boost/boost_it_N.csv): one Gaussian per mode, in box units, from the rows of that mode. rho(theta) is the smallest, over the modes, of the Mahalanobis distance to the mode’s Gaussian divided by the largest such distance among the mode’s own rows; inside means rho <= inflate. No network enters and nothing is assumed about the shape beyond one ellipsoid per mode.

restrict gives the surrogate a consumer should see: the network inside, and outside a value that does not look at the network at all, far below anything inside and falling away from the region, so that a search or a sampler started anywhere in the box is led to it.

Measured on the analytic targets (docs/boost.md): with inflate 2 none of 2e6 exact posterior draws lies outside (58 parameters, and the two-mode twin with 16), all known false maxima lie at 3 to 55 times the reach, and a nested sampler over the whole box returns the exact evidence within its error from a network with false maxima 2000 log-units above the best.

One ellipsoid per verified cloud of points (each (n, ndim)), reaching as far as its farthest point, or with quantile as that quantile of its points’ distances (acquisition.boost.region_quantile; which rows are one mode is still decided by the farthest).

A cloud with more than SPARSE * ndim distinct rows has its own covariance. A sparser one has no covariance to speak of: it takes the shape of a well-sampled cloud (_donor), its own centre, and reaches as far as its own rows do, never farther than the cloud whose shape it has. A cloud that cannot be given a shape (fewer than two distinct rows, or sparse with nothing to take a shape from) raises ValueError: shaped says which clouds can, and boost_region leaves the others out.

start(n, rng, component=None)#

n points inside the region and the box: starting walkers for a chain. With component, inside that ellipsoid’s cell (the points nearer to it than to any other). A cell can be a small part of its ellipsoid, one that lies inside another’s or is cut by one: the draw then closes in on the ellipsoid’s centre, which is in its own cell.

Without component the region can also reach far beyond the box, along parameters whose posterior fills their range or lies against a bound: 0.46% of the draws were in the box at network 18 of an axion run, whose farthest boost row had gone from 9.7 to 18.2 sigma, and 200 draws left the chain 70 walkers short. When 200 draws have not found n points, the draw closes in on the centres the same way. A draw that finds its points in those 200 is the one it always was, number for number.

sampling.region.boost_clouds(directory, names, before, best, depth, lower, upper)#

The verified posterior points of the boosts before iteration before, one cloud per mode.

A boost row counts when its true value is finite and within depth of the best: a row the network placed in its posterior and the truth did not is no posterior point. The modes of one boost are numbered heaviest first, which need not be the same order in the next: the first boost’s modes start the clouds. After that an old cloud joins the group of the current boost with which it shares the largest fraction of rows (its rows inside the group’s ellipsoid, or the group’s inside its own), provided the two are one mode (_one_mode: the smaller one’s mean inside the larger one’s reach); otherwise it stays as it is, as does a mode absent from this boost. Rows are assigned once, and several old clouds can join one group: a later boost can find as one mode what earlier ones found as two. Returns (clouds, info). Every cloud is returned, however few its rows: boost_region decides which can be given an ellipsoid.

sampling.region.boost_region(directory, names, lower, upper, inflate, before, best, depth, quantile=None)#

The Region of the boosts before iteration before, or (None, info) when there is none yet.

A cloud without a shape is left out and counted (info[“left_out”], its rows): a single verified point, or a sparse cloud while no cloud is well-sampled. It is not an error: a run whose boost has one verified point in a mode must be able to go on. (For a day a single point raised here, in the loop, from the saved boost files: at every restart.) info[“small_clouds”] counts the sparse clouds that are in, with a borrowed shape.
