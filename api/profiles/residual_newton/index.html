profiles.residual_newton#

Surrogate-corrected true-likelihood profile refinement, residual Newton method.

The true stage of every profile study.

Around an incumbent u_b (box coordinates, d = u - u_b) the truth is modelled as

t(u) ~ s(u) + r(u_b) + g . d + 0.5 d^T Q d,

with s the network (all its nonlinearity kept), g the gradient of the residual r = t - s, measured by finite differences, and Q a correction to the network’s curvature, learned and carried along the profile. Coordinates are whitened by the network’s local curvature (sigma capped at max_step box half-widths, as profiles.hybrid.curvature_geometry).

Per node (profiled coordinate fixed):

  1. Start at the surrogate optimum; its true value is the workflow’s direct call.

  2. Transfer: predict g from the previous nodes of the chain (the residual gradient at an optimum of t is exactly -grad s there; extrapolated linearly along the profile) and spend one call at the model’s maximum if it predicts more than transfer_min. A transfer that fails pauses transfers for two nodes.

  3. Gradient round, one concurrent batch: forward differences of t - s, step sigmas along the network’s curvature eigenvectors (capped at fd_max_box), inward along the axis for every free coordinate on a bound (even if the predicted gradient is outward), and central differences of flat_step sigmas along the flat_central flattest directions, which also measure the curvature there (written into Q). g is the whitened least-squares fit, ridged towards the prediction.

  4. Trust-region step on the model (radius starts at trust0 sigmas; the network is least reliable along its flattest direction). One call. Q is corrected along the step from the actual/predicted mismatch, and by a PSB secant update when the next gradient round is secant_min sigmas or more away. A good step to the edge of the region steps again with the gradient carried by Q; a failed one shrinks the region, and a failure within escalate sigmas mirrors every probe (central differences, full diagonal curvature).

  5. Stop when a freshly measured gradient predicts at most tol, or after a good step when the curvature error bound 0.5 (L * distance since the gradient round)^2 is below tol (L: the model’s curvature error, floored at curvature, learned from secant and step mismatches beyond the noise, carried along the chain with factor L_memory).

Each profile starts at its node nearest the surrogate’s best fit, with no prior, and runs two chains outward from it; a node learns only from its predecessors in its own chain, so results do not depend on timing or on workers. The global best fit is solved last, from the best node with every coordinate free. On resume, finished nodes are loaded from their checkpoints (each holds the state its successor needs) and an interrupted node is re-run; its journaled calls come back from the ledger’s cache, so no call is repeated.

The reported node value is always an evaluated true likelihood (a lower bound on the profile). Stopping is model-based and does not certify accuracy.

profiles.residual_newton.chain_plan(order, chain_length)#

Split the nodes on one side of a seed, nearest first, into the seed’s own chain and further (seed, chain) pieces: at most chain_length nodes are solved in sequence from one cold start. None keeps a single chain; 0 makes every node a seed.

profiles.residual_newton.refused_start(x_start, prior, **row)#

The row of a node whose start the likelihood refuses (a prior edge, a Boltzmann failure): no value, and the state it was given goes on along its chain, as from a singular node. Its checkpoint keeps it on a resume, since the objective is deterministic.

profiles.residual_newton.run_seeded_chains(pool, seeds, chains_of, run)#

Solve every seed at once, then each seed’s chains as soon as that seed is done (not when every seed is). run(k, order, state) returns (k, {j: row}); a chain starts from its seed’s final state. Returns {(k, j): row}. Which state a node starts from is fixed by the plan, so the rows do not depend on completion order.

profiles.residual_newton.refine_profiles_newton(model, source, bounds, ledger, out, config=None, log=print, direct=True, workers=1, surrogate=None, nodes=None, chain_length=None)#

The true stage of a 1D study: stage names corrected/{direct,node}/NAME/J. nodes (grid indices) restricts the refinement to a subset; the others are reported as null. chain_length caps how many nodes are solved in sequence from one cold start (None: two chains per profile, outward from its seed; see chain_plan).
